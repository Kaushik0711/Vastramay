# 11 — Content, Product Assets and Brand Rules

## 1. Brand name

The project and website name is **Vastramay**. Search for stale “Rose Orchid” references in:
- Header and footer wordmark
- Page titles and metadata
- Announcement bar and newsletter copy
- Empty states and image placeholders
- Emails and order confirmations
- Admin configuration
- README and documentation
- Asset filenames or graphics

Replace stale names unless explicitly retained as a collection name or design reference. Do not assume a logo file exists; inspect the asset directory.

## 2. Product photography

- Use genuine product photos supplied or approved by the business.
- Keep the photo style and cropping coherent across a collection.
- Use the right focal point for garments; avoid cutting off important clothing details.
- Use alternate views only when they are actually available.
- Avoid displaying a product photo from another item.
- Avoid AI-generated imagery presented as real product photography.
- Use a clear fallback when an image is missing.
- Provide meaningful alt text.

## 3. Product data accuracy

Use actual data for:
- Name
- Category
- Description
- Price
- Compare-at price
- Variant size/color
- SKU
- Stock
- Image list
- Availability
- Discounts
- Ratings/reviews if supported by genuine data

Never fabricate stock urgency, “best seller” status, sales counts, customer reviews or discounts. When sales data is unavailable, use a manually curated featured collection and label it accordingly.

## 4. Homepage content

Suggested section purposes:
1. Hero — broad brand promise accurately reflecting the catalogue.
2. Benefits strip — only real store benefits, such as actual shipping/returns terms.
3. New Arrivals — products that are actually new.
4. Shop by Category — representative imagery for each category.
5. Editorial campaign — unique imagery and a clear story.
6. Best Sellers — real sales data or clearly curated alternatives.
7. Brand story — verified brand information.
8. Newsletter — actual subscription integration.
9. Footer — support, policies and verified social links.

Avoid repeating the same prominent image in multiple sections unless there is a deliberate art-direction reason.

## 5. Copy style

- Concise, confident, fashion-aware.
- Avoid exaggerated luxury claims without supporting evidence.
- Do not invent a brand founder, manufacturing origin, ethical certification or company history.
- Make CTA labels clear and action-oriented.
- Keep product details factual and useful.

## 6. Business details still to confirm

- Final logo/wordmark
- Exact target audience and product category scope
- Official brand story
- Real product catalogue and images
- Size chart and fit guidance
- Shipping/returns/cancellation rules
- Support email and contact details
- Social account URLs
- Newsletter provider
- Merchant/payment account
- Shipping provider
- Legal business details and tax requirements
